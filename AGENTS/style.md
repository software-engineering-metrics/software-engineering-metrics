# House style

The enforceable version is
[`locales/en-gb-oxendict/contributing/style-rules.md`](../locales/en-gb-oxendict/contributing/style-rules.md)
and [`spec/conventions.md`](../spec/conventions.md). The tests check most of it.

- **No em-dashes.** Never use U+2014. Use a comma, colon, parentheses, or two
  sentences. En-dashes are allowed only in numeric ranges.
- **No stock LLM phrasing.** No "not only ... but also", "but also",
  "load-bearing", "It's important to note", "In today's fast-paced world".
- **Follow the topic template** in
  [`chapter-template.md`](../locales/en-gb-oxendict/contributing/chapter-template.md).
- **Define terms on first use** and **link key concepts to Wikipedia** on first
  mention. Real references only; never fabricate a work or a URL.
- **Name the gaming vector.** A topic that presents a metric without how it gets
  gamed and what pairs with it to catch that is not finished.
- **Spelling:** author in `en-gb-oxendict` (British English, Oxford spelling),
  per [`spec/oxford-spelling.md`](../spec/oxford-spelling.md).
