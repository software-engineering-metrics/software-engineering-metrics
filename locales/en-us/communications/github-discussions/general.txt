Say hello and tell us how you use Software Engineering Metrics

Welcome to the Software Engineering Metrics Discussions. This is the place for introductions, opinions, and open conversation about the book.

I am a maintainer of the project. I wrote Software Engineering Metrics because I wanted a working book about measuring software engineering well, covering the Flow Framework, the SPACE framework, queueing theory, DORA metrics, and how generative AI changes what the numbers mean to be easy to find and use. I would like to hear who is reading it and why.

**One question to start:** Which software engineering metric does your team track today, and does it reflect real outcomes?

A sentence or two is plenty. Replies to other people are welcome too.
